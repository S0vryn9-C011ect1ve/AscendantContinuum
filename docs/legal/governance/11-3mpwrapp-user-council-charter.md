**Created:** 2026-02-26  
**Status:** TEMPLATE (Elections when 3mpwrApp reaches public launch)  
**Election Timeline:** TBD (dependent on user base reaching 500+ active users)

---

## 🎯 Purpose

The **3mpwrApp Community User Council** represents the voice of disability community members, allies, and caregivers using 3mpwrApp. It provides real-time feedback on platform decisions, moderates community spaces, and elects delegates to the Cross-Platform Governance Board.

**This Council does NOT exist yet.** It will be formed at 3mpwrApp public launch (target Q1-Q2 2026), when there is a sufficient active user base.

---

## 👥 Composition

### **Council Size: 5 members**

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|-----------------|
| **Chair** | Leads meetings, represents council to founder + board |
| **Co-Chair** | Supports chair, leads specific committee |
| **Treasurer** | Tracks any budget allocations to council (future) |
| **Secretary** | Records decisions, manages confidential docs |
| **Communications Lead** | Communicates with community, publishes updates |

### **Member Eligibility**

**Must meet ALL:**
- Active 3mpwrApp user for 30+ days
- Disability lived experience **OR** demonstrated ally (2+ years advocacy work)
- No conflict of interest (not founder, not current staff)
- Willing to serve 1-year term, meet monthly, respond to urgent issues
- Located in North America or Europe (timezone flexibility)

---

## 🗳️ Election Process

### **When Elections Happen**

**First election:** When 3mpwrApp reaches 500+ active users (founder announces date)

**Ongoing:** Every 12 months, 3 of 5 seats up for rotation
- Staggered: 2 seats rotate Year 1, 2 seats Year 2, 1 seat Year 3, repeat
- Members can run for re-election

### **Who Votes?**

- All 3mpwrApp users with 30+ days active on platform
- **Vote weight:**
  - Disabled users = **2 votes** each
  - Ally/caregiver users = **1 vote** each
- **Threshold:** Top 5 candidates with highest vote counts win seats

### **Nomination & Vetting (15-day process)**

**Days 1-5: Nominations**
- Open call: "Who should represent 3mpwrApp community?"
- Self-nominations only
- Nominees provide: 200-word statement + disability/ally credentials

**Days 6-10: Community vetting**
- Nominees answer public Q&A in forum
- Community shares concerns (if any)
- Founder vetoes if conflict of interest discovered

**Days 11-15: Voting**
- All eligible users vote
- Ranked-choice voting (prevents spoiler effects)
- Top 5 become Council members

## 📋 Responsibilities

### **Daily**

- **Moderate community spaces** (Discord, forums, in-app messaging):
  - Remove spam, harassment, off-topic posts
  - Enforce community guidelines (be respectful, no ableism, no discrimination)
  - Flag urgent safety issues (crisis helpline, abuse reports) to founder

- **Respond to user questions** (within 24 hours):
  - How do I use feature X?
  - Can you review my profile / evidence?
  - I reported being harassed, what now?

### **Weekly**

- **Check-in meeting** (30 min, async chat or video):
  - Summary of week's moderation actions
  - Any patterns noticed (bugs, user pain points, features requested)
  - Escalations to founder (if needed)

### **Monthly**

- **Full Council meeting** (1 hour):
  - Larger issues from past month
  - User feedback themes
  - Recommendations to founder
  - Reporting to Cross-Platform Board (when formed)

### **Quarterly**

- **Community townhall** (facilitated by council):
  - Founder presents updates + decisions made last quarter
  - Users ask questions
  - Council collects feedback for next quarter

---

## 📊 Decision-Making Authority

### **What the Council Can Decide (No founder approval needed)**

- ✅ Remove abusive user (after documented warnings)
- ✅ Edit/remove off-topic post from community space
- ✅ Answer user support questions
- ✅ Organize community events (meetups, contests, feedback sessions)
- ✅ Recommend features to founder (can vocalize user needs)
- ✅ Recommend policy clarifications (what does "respectful" mean?)

### **What Requires Founder/Board Approval**

- ❌ Change to core platform features
- ❌ Monetization changes (pricing, new paid features)
- ❌ Major policy changes (accessibility, privacy, ToS)
- ❌ User data access (cannot see evidence without warrant / legal request)
- ❌ Banning user (except for immediate safety; founder reviews after)

### **Escalation Path**

```
User issue detected
  ↓
Council addresses (if in their authority)
  ↓
If unresolved or requires founder decision:
  ↓
Council recommends to founder (weekly summary)
  ↓
Founder decides + communicates back
  ↓
If founder decision seems wrong: Council can appeal to Cross-Platform Board (Phase 3+)
```

---

## 🛡️ Safeguards

### **Can Council Be Removed?**

**Individual member removed if:**
- Missing 2 consecutive monthly meetings without excuse
- Conflict of interest discovered
- Community votes them out (2/3 vote)
- Gross misconduct (harassment, data breach, etc.)

**Entire council removed if:**
- Requires 7/9 Cross-Platform Board vote (Phase 3+)
- Grounds: Systematic abuse of power, refusing to moderate harassment, etc.
- Emergency election held within 2 weeks

### **Term Limits**

- Serve 1-year term
- Can be re-elected maximum 2 times (3 years total)
- Then must sit out 1 year before running again (prevents dynasty)

---

## 💬 Communication

### **Public Council Updates**

**Monthly public summary** (posted to discord + in-app):
- Major moderation actions taken
- User feedback themes this month
- Upcoming features or decisions
- How to contact council with feedback

**Quarterly public meeting minutes**:
- What council discussed
- What recommendations went to founder
- Why certain decisions were made
- Link to townhall video

### **Confidentiality**

**Council members keep confidential:**
- Personal details of users in crisis
- Identities of anyone reporting abuse
- Sensitive business discussions
- Disputes between council members (present unified front publicly)

**Council members can publicize:**
- General feedback themes (e.g., "many users requesting offline mode")
- Approved founder decisions + rationale
- Community guidelines & their enforcement

---

## 🚨 Emergency Authority

**If urgent safety issue arises** (user in acute crisis, active harassment campaign, etc.):

- Council chair can take immediate action (remove posts, temporarily restrict user, notify founder)
- No waiting for full council vote
- Report action at next meeting + founder gets transparency

---

## 📈 Success Metrics

**The Council is thriving if:**
- ✅ Monthly meetings happen on schedule with 4/5 attendance
- ✅ User feedback gets addressed within 30 days
- ✅ Community sentiment remains positive (survey >70% trust)
- ✅ Moderation keeps community safe (harassment reports decrease)
- ✅ Council recommendations are heard + considered by founder
- ✅ User suggestions implemented within 1 quarter (or declined with explanation)
