## 🎯 Key Architecture

### Current Phase (Phase 1-2: Bootstrap)
- Founder manages S0vryn9 finances + strategy
- Primary Backend Trustee named later (TBD)
- User Councils planned at platform launch
- Cross-Platform Governance Board forms at Phase 3

### Phase 3+ (When Mutual Aid Foundation Exists)
- Founder reports quarterly to Cross-Platform Board
- 3-Trustee Oversight Model: Primary (founder-appointed) + #2 & #3 (board-elected)
- 3 User Councils provide real-time community feedback
- 9-member Governance Board makes ecosystem-wide decisions

### Emergency Activation (Dead Man's Switch Triggers)
If founder becomes unavailable (90-day inactivity, death, medical incapacity, or voluntary transition):
1. **Phase 1 (Days 1-7):** 3-trustee team stabilizes all systems (read-only mode)
2. **Phase 2 (Days 8-45):** Community elects Emergency Council or formal board
3. **Phase 3 (Days 31-60):** Access handoff, financial transition, multi-sig rules deployed
4. **Phase 4 (Day 61+):** Ongoing governance with quarterly cycles

## 🛡️ 3-Trustee Oversight Model

**The 3-Trustee team during emergency:**
- **Primary Trustee** (founder-appointed): Leads technical response, infrastructure continuity
- **Trustee #2** (board-elected): Verification, ensures board direction followed, audits actions
- **Trustee #3** (board-elected): Ethical oversight, community accountability, policy veto power

**All 3 must agree on:**
- Infrastructure freeze (Day 1 emergency)
- Major financial transitions (Days 36-40)
- Deploying multi-signature rules (Days 41-45)
- Resuming platform operations (Days 46-50)
- Any decision that overrides board direction

**Prevents:** Single trustee unilateral action, board pressure on trustee, any one voice having sole power

## 🎮 Ascendant-Specific Governance

**Your platform has a unique protection:**

The **Ascendant User Council** can **unilaterally block** any proposal that violates the "no-pay-to-win" doctrine:
- ✅ Cannot be overridden by founder without board approval + public justification
- ✅ Protects gameplay fairness (disabled players don't have to pay for competitive advantage)
- ✅ Community's last defense against greedy design decisions

**Your council includes:**
- 3 disabled game players (accessibility-first approach)
- 2 allies from the community
- Dedicated accessibility member who can call emergency sessions if barriers discovered

**Examples of doctrine violations (council can veto):**
- ❌ "Limited cosmetics gated behind 40 hrs/week grind" (time-gates disadvantage disabled players)
- ❌ "Loot box mechanical advantage" (pay = power)
- ❌ "Exclusive weapons behind $9.99 paywall" (pay-to-win)

✅ **Allowed:** Cosmetic skins, battle pass cosmetics, founders pack with cosmetics only

## ✨ Voting Weights

All elections use **accessibility-first weighting:**
- **PWD (People With Disabilities):** 2 votes each
- **Allies:** 1 vote each

This ensures disability perspectives are represented at scale in governance.
