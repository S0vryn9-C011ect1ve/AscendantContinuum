# Mutual Aid Foundation Community User Council Charter

**Version:** 1.0  
**Created:** 2026-02-26  
**Status:** TEMPLATE (Formed when Foundation is established, Phase 3)  
**Election Timeline:** Upon foundation establishment (target 2026-Q3 or later)

---

## 🎯 Purpose

The **Mutual Aid Foundation Community User Council** represents the voices of beneficiaries (people receiving aid), donors/supporters, and disability advocacy organizations. It ensures that the foundation operates with integrity, allocates funds fairly, and remains accountable to the disability community it serves.

**This Council will be formed when the Mutual Aid Foundation launches (Phase 3).** Until then, foundation planning is founder-led in consultation with disability advocates.

---

## 👥 Composition

### **Council Size: 5 members**

| Seat | Representative | Selection |
|------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Beneficiary#1** | Current/recent foundation grant recipient (disability-lived) | Elected by beneficiaries |
| **Beneficiary#2** | Another current/recent recipient (different disability) | Elected by beneficiaries |
| **Advocate** | Disability advocacy org representative (ally or lived experience) | Nominated by disability orgs, voted by community |
| **Community Partner** | Representative from partner org (legal aid, crisis, etc.) | Partnering org nominates |
| **Coordination Lead** | Facilitator (could be founder or external; coordinates/documents) | Founder appoints |

### **Member Eligibility**

**Beneficiary seats (2):**
- Within last 18 months received foundation grant
- Disabled or family member of disabled person
- Willing to share lived experience
- Commitment to help other beneficiaries access aid

**Advocate seat (1):**
- 2+ years disability advocacy or community organizing
- Or disabled person with formal peer support training
- Represents broader disability movement (not just foundation)

**Community Partner seat (1):**
- Representative from partner organization (legal aid, housing, crisis line)
- Deep relationships with disabled community
- Brings external perspective to foundation decisions

**Coordination Lead (1):**
- Organizational skills + trustworthiness
- Neutral mediator (not advocating one position over others)
- Can be replaced if role becomes too burdensome

## 🗳️ Selection Process

### **When Elections Happen**

**First formation:** Upon foundation establishment (Phase 3)

**Annual rotation:** 1-2 seats rotate each year (keeps continuity)

### **Beneficiary Seat Elections** (Only beneficiaries vote)

**Timing:** Every 12 months, both seats rotate

**Who votes:** Anyone who received foundation grant in past 18 months

**Process:**
- Open nominations (self or peer-nominated)
- 1-week public vetting
- Vote: Top 2 candidates become beneficiary seats

### **Advocate Seat Election** (All community votes)

**Who votes:** All foundation supporters (donors, beneficiaries, disability org partners)

**Process:**
- Disability organizations submit nominee
- Public vetting (review org track record, nominee integrity)
- Vote: Highest vote-getter wins seat

### **Community Partner Seat** (Partner org + founder negotiation)

**Process:**
- Founder/board identifies key partner organization
- Partner org suggests representative
- Mutual agreement, 1-year appointment
- Can be renewed or rotated

## 📋 Responsibilities

### **Daily/Weekly**

- **Be available to beneficiaries:** Answer questions about grant process, appeal decisions, access support
- **Monitor foundation communications:** Social media, emails, feedback requests
- **Collect stories:** What's working? What's broken? How did the grant help you?

### **Monthly**

- **Council meeting** (1 hour):
  - Grant decisions from past month
  - Any beneficiary complaints or praise
  - Trends noticed (X types of grants more successful, Y barriers emerging)
  - Accessibility of application process (easy to apply? Language barriers?)

### **Quarterly**

- **Community townhall** (2 hours):
  - Foundation staff presents quarterly impact report
  - Council members share feedback themes
  - Beneficiaries + community ask questions
  - Vote on any major policy changes (advisory, council decides)

### **Annually**

- **Impact audit:**
  - Review sample of grants (did they help? Did money reach intended person?)
  - Check: Are policies being followed? Any corruption/misuse?
  - Transparency report: How many people served? Total $ distributed? What's it going toward?

## 💰 Authority & Decision-Making

### **What Council Can Decide**

- ✅ Recommend grant allocations for next quarter
- ✅ Suggest policy changes (eligibility, grant sizes, application process)
- ✅ Veto suspicious grant request (if signs of fraud)
- ✅ Recommend staff changes (if someone is rude/ableist to beneficiaries)
- ✅ Ensure grants go to most vulnerable (not just "easiest" cases)
- ✅ Create support programs for beneficiaries (peer support, buddy system)

### **What Requires Cross-Platform Board/Founder Approval**

- ❌ Major budget decisions (how much total to allocate that year)
- ❌ Hiring/firing foundation staff
- ❌ Partnership agreements with other orgs
- ❌ Policy that contradicts disability-led principles
- ❌ Anything involving legal liability

### **Escalation Matrix**

```
Beneficiary files complaint (grant denied unfairly, etc.)
  ↓
Council reviews + investigates
  ↓
If council agrees complaint is valid:
  ↓
Council overturns decision OR recommends appeal process to board
  ↓
Board must respond within 30 days (approve council or explain why not)
```

## 🛡️ Safeguards

### **Council Member Removal**

**Individual removed if:**
- Missing 2 consecutive monthly meetings
- Misuses confidential information (breaks beneficiary privacy)
- Conflict of interest discovered (e.g., beneficiary approves own relative's grant)
- Financial misconduct

**Entire Council removed if:**
- Requires 7/9 Cross-Platform Board vote
- Grounds: Systematic abuse, falsifying grant information, fraud

### **Term Limits**

- Beneficiary seats: Annual rotation (1 year, then new beneficiary elected)
- Advocate seat: 2-year term, can re-run once (2 max)
- Partner seat: 1-year, renewable annually
- Coordination lead: Annual review (can be replaced)

---

## 📋 Beneficiary Privacy & Dignity

### **Confidentiality**

**Council keeps strictly confidential:**
- Names of grant recipients (except if they volunteer publicly)
- Personal details of situation (why they needed aid)
- Grant amounts to specific individuals
- Any health/disability information shared

**Council can publicize (with permission):**
- Aggregate stats (X total beneficiaries, Y avg grant amount, Z outcomes)
- De-identified stories (with beneficiary's written permission)
- Impact themes (e.g., "60% of grants went to cover rent after job loss")

### **Dignity-Centered Process**

**Council ensures:**
- Application process is accessible (available in plain language, multiple formats)
- No assumptions about disabled people ("you need X because disabled")
- Beneficiaries treated with respect, not judgment
- Fast processing (people in crisis need help NOW, not in 3 months)
- Appeals process exists (grant denied? Can ask for reconsideration)

## 📊 Success Metrics

**The Council is thriving if:**
- ✅ Monthly meetings happen with 4/5 attendance
- ✅ Grant applications are processed within 2 weeks
- ✅ Beneficiaries report respectful treatment (survey >85% satisfied)
- ✅ Grants are actually reaching the people intended
- ✅ Council recommendations are heard + implemented
- ✅ No financial impropriety detected (audit passes)
- ✅ Disability community views foundation as trustworthy (reputation grows)

## 🔄 Relationship to S0vryn9 Financial Flows

**Council understands where the money comes from:**
- S0vryn9 takes all revenue from 3mpwrApp + Ascendant
- A defined percentage (founder reports quarterly to board) goes to foundation
- Foundation Council decides how to allocate that amount
- Council has NO authority over founder's business decisions (what % to allocate), but can recommend

**Example:**
- Q1 revenue: $50,000
- Founder allocates 20% ($10,000) to foundation
- Foundation Council receives $10,000 + decides which grants to fund
- Council CANNOT order founder to allocate 30%
- Council CAN lobby Cross-Platform Board to recommend higher allocation
