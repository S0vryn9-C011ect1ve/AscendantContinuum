## 🎯 Purpose

The **Ascendant Continuum Community User Council** represents players of the game. It protects the foundational doctrine (no pay-to-win, no loot boxes, cosmetics-only monetization, ethical gameplay), provides feedback on game balance and features, and elects delegates to the Cross-Platform Governance Board.

**This Council does NOT exist yet.** It will be formed at Ascendant Continuum public beta launch, when there is sufficient active player base.

---

## 👥 Composition

### **Council Size: 5 members**

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|-----------------|
| **Chair** | Leads meetings, represents players to founder + board |
| **Co-Chair/Game Design Lead** | Gathers gameplay feedback, represents balance concerns |
| **Accessibility Lead** | Ensures game remains accessible to disabled players |
| **Community Lead** | Runs events, manages player social spaces |
| **Secretary** | Records decisions, manages meeting notes |

### **Member Eligibility**

**Must meet ALL:**
- Active Ascendant player for 60+ days (because game mastery takes time)
- For 3/5 seats: Disability lived experience preferred (PWD players voice needs)
- For 2/5 seats: Ally players acceptable (non-disability but skilled/trusted player)
- No conflict of interest (not founder, not developer, not paid staff)
- Willing to serve 1-year term, attend monthly meetings, play 5+ hours/week
- Agree to uphold core doctrine (cosmetics-only, no p2w, no exploitation)

---

## 🗳️ Election Process

### **When Elections Happen**

**First election:** When Ascendant reaches 500+ active players (public beta stage)

**Ongoing:** Every 12 months, 3 of 5 seats rotate

### **Who Votes?**

- All Ascendant players with 60+ days playtime
- **Vote weight:**
  - Disabled players = **2 votes** each
  - Ally players = **1 vote** each
- Special consideration: High-level players (cosmetic-earned rewards) get slightly more voice in balance discussions (but equal voting)

### **Nomination & Vetting (15-day process)**

**Days 1-5: Open nominations**
- Players nominate themselves
- Required: Statement (300 words) + why they should represent players + gameplay philosophy
- List: In-game achievements (level, playtime, community contributions)

**Days 6-10: Community vetting**
- Public forum: Players comment on nominees
- Red flags: Anyone advocating for pay-to-win, loot boxes, or predatory mechanics
- Founder can veto candidates who don't respect core doctrine

**Days 11-15: Voting**
- All eligible players vote
- Top 5 become Council members
- Tiebreaker: Disability-led weighting (if two players tie, PWD player wins seat)

## 📋 Responsibilities

### **Daily**

- **Monitor community spaces** (Discord, forums, in-game chat, Reddit):
  - Flag toxic behavior or harassment (no tolerance for hate speech)
  - Answer player questions: How do I...? Where do I find...? What's the meta?
  - Watch for exploits ou bugs, report to development team

- **Protect core doctrine:**
  - Call out anyone suggesting pay-to-win features
  - Ensure cosmetic shop stays cosmetics-only (no hidden mechanics)
  - Monitor battle pass / seasonal passes (do they favor disabled players?)

### **Weekly**

- **Player council sync** (30 min):
  - Week's community feedback themes
  - Bugs reported + severity
  - Balance issues (is X character overpowered?)
  - Accessibility concerns (any players reporting difficulty?)

### **Monthly**

- **Full Council meeting** (1 hour):
  - Major gameplay balance proposals
  - Community sentiment / retention metrics
  - Feature requests (what do players want?)
  - Recommendations to development team
  - Report to Cross-Platform Board (when formed)

### **Quarterly**

- **Public player townhall** (2 hours, recorded):
  - Council shares feedback collected last quarter
  - Development team explains changes made + why
  - Live Q&A with players
  - Vote on balance proposal (advisory, founder + dev team make final call)

## 📊 Decision-Making Authority

### **What Council Can Decide**

- ✅ Remove toxic player from community spaces (after warnings)
- ✅ Host in-game events (tournaments, seasonal challenges)
- ✅ Recommend balance changes (X class too strong, Y weapon underused)
- ✅ Suggest accessibility improvements (font too small, colors hard to see)
- ✅ Create player guides, wiki contributions
- ✅ Organize clan meetups, community discords
- ✅ Recommend cosmetic themes (but cannot approve monetization strategy)

### **What Requires Developer/Founder Approval**

- ❌ Change to core game mechanics
- ❌ New cosmetic shop items (founder/dev team approves to ensure stays cosmetics-only)
- ❌ Seasonal battle pass structure (council advises, founder decides)
- ❌ Cross-platform features (council advises, technical team decides)
- ❌ Player account bans (council can recommend, founder has final authority)

### **The "Doctrine Veto"**

**Council has unilateral power to veto ANY proposal that violates core doctrine:**
- ✅ Pay-to-win mechanics proposed → **Council blocks** (founder must explain why he/she wants to break the doctrine, board decides)
- ✅ Loot boxes suggested → **Council blocks immediately**
- ✅ Cosmetics price gouging → **Council recommends review** (founder can override, but must justify publicly)

## ♿ Accessibility Focus

### **Accessibility Council Member (Dedicated Role)**

This council member is always a **disabled player** and focuses entirely on:
- Game remains playable for colorblind players
- Text sizes work for low vision players
- Controls work for players with mobility issues
- Audio descriptions available for deaf/hard-of-hearing
- Pacing doesn't create sensory overload issues

**This member can call an emergency council session** if they discover accessibility breaking.

---

## 🛡️ Safeguards

### **Can Council Be Removed?**

**Individual member removed if:**
- Missing 2 consecutive monthly meetings
- Advocates for pay-to-win or exploitation
- Engages in harassment or abuse
- Serious conflict of interest discovered

**Entire Council removed if:**
- Requires 7/9 Cross-Platform Board vote (Phase 3+)
- Grounds: Failure to protect 'no pay-to-win' doctrine, systematic misconduct

### **Term Limits**

- Serve 1 year
- Can be re-elected max 2 times (3 years total)
- Must sit out 1 year before running again

## 💬 Communication

### **Public Updates**

**Weekly summary** (posted to Discord + forum):
- Feedback themes from players
- Bugs being tracked
- Upcoming balance changes (if any)
- How to reach council with concerns

**Quarterly public meeting minutes**:
- What was discussed
- Why certain decisions were made
- Upcoming priorities
- Links to meeting recording + townhall

### **Confidentiality**

**Keep confidential:**
- Personal details of players reporting harassment
- Unreleased features/roadmap (until founder announces)
- Individual disputes between players
- Any business-sensitive revenue information

**Can publicize:**
- General balance discussions + reasoning
- Accessibility improvements
- Community sentiment (feeling of fairness)
- Doctrine defense (e.g., "we turned down pay-to-win monetization because it violates our founding principle")

## 🎮 Example: Doctrine Veto in Action

**Scenario:** Developer proposes limited-time cosmetics that can only be earned by playing 40+ hours per week. Council says:

> "This violates our doctrine. Cosmetics should NEVER create FOMO or require extreme time commitment. Disabled players can't play 40 hours/week. This is pay-to-win via time pressure. We veto."

**Founder's options:**
1. Accept council veto → cosmetic changed to be earnable by casual players
2. Override veto → must explain publicly why AND board must review (could be overridden by board)
3. Modify proposal → make cosmetic earnable but with optional paid speedup (costs extra $ if you want it faster, not required)

**Council gets final say on doctrine matters.**

## 📈 Success Metrics

**The Council is thriving if:**
- ✅ Monthly meetings happen with 4/5 attendance
- ✅ No pay-to-win or predatory mechanics slip into game
- ✅ Player retention is stable/growing (community feels respected)
- ✅ Accessibility improves each quarter (more players can play)
- ✅ Quarterly townhalls have >80% positive sentiment
- ✅ Council recommendations are implemented or explained within 30 days
- ✅ Disabled players survey shows game is "genuinely accessible" (not tokenized)
