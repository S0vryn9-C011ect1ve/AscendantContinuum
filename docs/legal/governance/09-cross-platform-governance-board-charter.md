# Cross-Platform Governance Board Charter

**Version:** 1.0  
**Created:** 2026-02-26  
**Effective:** Upon formation (Phase 3+)  
**Owner:** Founder (reports to board quarterly)

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Authority

The **Cross-Platform Governance Board** is the decision-making body for the S0vryn9 C011ect1ve ecosystem. It represents the voices of three independent User Councils (3mpwrApp, Ascendant Continuum, Mutual Aid Foundation) and ensures alignment on ethical principles, financial flows, and community welfare.

**This board does NOT exist yet.** It forms when the Mutual Aid Foundation is established (Phase 3). Until then, governance is founder-directed with User Councils providing feedback.

## 👥 Composition

### **Board Membership (9 total)**

| Platform | Delegates | Elected By |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| 3mpwrApp | 3 members | 3mpwrApp User Council (elected by community PWD 2x, Allies 1x) |
| Ascendant Continuum | 3 members | Ascendant User Council (elected by community PWD 2x, Allies 1x) |
| Mutual Aid Foundation | 3 members | Foundation User Council (elected by beneficiaries + allies PWD 2x, Allies 1x) |

**Total Voting Members:** 9

**Founder (Ex-Officio, Non-Voting):**
- Attends all meetings
- Reports quarterly on finances + strategy
- Cannot vote, but can speak and answer questions
- Reports directly to board on S0vryn9 operations (finance, infrastructure, hiring)

**Backend Trustees (Advisors, Non-Voting in Normal Governance):**
- **Primary Trustee:** Founder-appointed (named later); attends quarterly meetings
- **Trustee #2 & #3:** Elected by Board at Phase 3+ (1 from each platform's User Council leaders)
- Reports on infrastructure health + security
- On-call for emergency sessions
- **During Dead Man's Switch Activation:** All 3 trustees become voting governance team (see "Emergency Activation" below)

## 🚨 Emergency Activation & 3-Trustee Oversight

### **When the Dead Man's Switch Protocol Activates**

If the founder becomes incapacitated, dies, or voluntarily transitions, governance changes immediately:

**The 3-Trustee Oversight Model activates:**

| Trustee | Appointment | Authority |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| **Primary** | Founder-appointed (now) | Leads technical response + infrastructure continuity |
| **#2** | Elected by Board; represents election process integrity | Ensures board direction followed; independent verification |
| **#3** | Elected by Board; represents ethical oversight | Ethical review + community accountability verification |

**Collectively, the 3 trustees during emergency:**
- Oversee entire ecosystem stabilization (Phases 1-3 of dead man's protocol)
- Direct Emergency Council/Governance Board election process (Phase 2)
- Approve all major decisions: **Requires 3/3 trustee agreement** (not just board majority)
- Manage financial transitions + platform transitions
- Report daily to community on activation status
- Expand governance board and user councils to full operational capacity
- Enforce multi-signature rules: Board decisions + trustee verification both required

**Board structure during emergency (Days 8-60):**
- **Emergency Council** (9 members): Hastily-elected platform leaders, reports to 3-trustee team
- **3-Trustee Oversight Team** (co-signers on all major decisions)
- Eventually transitions to: Fully elected 9-member permanent board + permanent trustee team

### **Board Expansion Trigger**

If an Emergency Council member becomes unavailable or proves unsuitable:
- Board can expand to **12-15 members** (add alternates/backups per platform)
- Expansion requires vote by remaining council members + approval from all 3 trustees
- Temporary expansion (returns to 9 after permanent board elected at Phase 3+)

## 📋 Member Selection & Term

### **Initial Election (Formation of Phase 3)**

1. **When:** Upon establishment of Mutual Aid Foundation (target: Q3 2026)
2. **Who votes:** All active users on each platform (PWD 2x, Allies 1x weighting)
3. **Eligibility:** 
   - Must be active user for 1+ year on their platform
   - Disability lived experience OR 2+ years documented ally work
   - No conflict of interest (cannot be founder or current staff)
   - Willing to serve 3 years (legal commitment)

### **Term Duration**

- **Each member serves: 3 years**
- **Rotation:** Staggered replacements (create stability)
  - Year 1: 3 members elected (1 per platform)
  - Year 1.5: 3 new members elected (1 per platform)
  - Year 2: 3 new members elected (1 per platform)
  - Repeat cycle
- **Re-election:** Members can run for second term if community votes to retain

### **Removal**

**Member can be removed if:**
1. **Community vote of no-confidence** (2/3 majority of their platform's users)
2. **Board-initiated removal** (6/9 vote) for:
   - Conflict of interest discovered
   - Missing 3 consecutive meetings without excuse
   - Public harassment or unethical conduct
   - Breach of confidentiality

**Process:**
- 30-day notice + public hearing
- Member can respond to charges
- Vote held Day 31
- If removed, replacement election within 15 days

## 🏛️ Board Governance Structure

### **Officers (Elected from within Board)**

**Board Chair**
- Selected by 6/9 vote (supermajority)
- 1-year term, can be re-elected
- Responsibilities:
  - Call meetings (regular + emergency)
  - Set agendas (in consultation with members)
  - Represent board publicly
  - Sign legal documents on behalf of ecosystem
  - Tie-breaker vote if split decision

**Vice Chair**
- Selected by 6/9 vote
- Substitutes for Chair if unavailable
- Leads specific committee (see below)

**Treasurer**
- Tracks funding flows (S0vryn9 → platforms + foundation)
- Prepares quarterly financial reports
- Ensures accuracy of all fund allocations
- Audits payment processors

**Secretary**
- Records meeting minutes (verbatim voting only, no names)
- Archives decisions in governance log
- Manages confidential documents storage
- Publishes quarterly public summary

---

### **Board Committees**

**Finance Committee** (Treasurer + 2 other members)
- Reviews all fund allocations quarterly
- Recommends % split between: 3mpwrApp infra, Ascendant infra, foundation, founder compensation, staff hiring
- Proposes emergency budget adjustments if needed
- Reports to full board monthly

**Policy Committee** (1 member per platform)
- Monitors policy consistency across platforms
- Reviews conflicts (if Ascendant policy contradicts 3mpwrApp policy)
- Proposes ecosystem-wide standards (accessibility, privacy, moderation)
- Escalates to full board if issues cannot be resolved

**Ethics & Community Committee** (3 members rotating annual chairs)
- Reviews user complaints about governance decisions
- Ensures disability-led principles are maintained
- Recommends board actions if ethical violations found
- Hosts quarterly community townhalls

## 📅 Meeting Schedule & Procedures

### **Regular Meetings**

**Quarterly Board Meeting** (In-person or video, ~4 hours)
- **When:** March 31, June 30, Sept 30, Dec 31 (+ 1 week flexibility)
- **Location:** Rotates between three platforms (so 3mpwrApp → Ascendant → Foundation → repeat)
- **Attendance:** All 9 members required (remote option available)
- **Quorum:** 6/9 members present (required to make binding decisions)

**Monthly Finance Check-in** (Finance Committee, 1 hour)
- Review S0vryn9 transactions
- Approve any payouts over $X threshold
- Report to full board

**Monthly User Council Sync** (1 member per platform + full board, 1 hour)
- User Councils report: Major user feedback, moderation issues, feature requests
- Board translates to strategic priorities
- Ensures boards hearing from ground level

### **Meeting Agenda Format**

**Standard quarterly meeting (~4 hours):**

1. **Opening (15 min)**
   - Chair calls to order
   - Roll call (name + platform)
   - Agenda review

2. **Founder Report (30 min)**
   - S0vryn9 financial summary (revenue, expenses, runway)
   - Backend Trustee infrastructure report
   - Strategic updates (partnerships, hiring, etc.)
   - Q&A from board

3. **Finance Review (45 min)**
   - Treasurer presents quarterly financial report
   - Allocation proposals for next quarter
   - Multi-sig vote on allocation %
   - Emergency fund requests (if any)

4. **Policy & Ethics Discussion (60 min)**
   - Policy Committee: Any inconsistencies across platforms?
   - Ethics Committee: User complaints or governance issues?
   - Platform-specific updates (what's new in 3mpwrApp, Ascendant, Foundation)

5. **Community Feedback (30 min)**
   - Summary of townhall feedback from last quarter
   - User survey results
   - Sentiment analysis (are users happy?)

6. **Strategic Decisions (optional, 45 min)**
   - Major partnership proposals
   - Policy changes requiring 3/5 vote
   - Board member elections (if rotation year)

7. **Closing (15 min)**
   - Secretary confirms action items + owners
   - Next meeting date set
   - Public summary draft (what can we announce?)

---

### **Voting & Decision-Making**

| Decision Type | Threshold | Notes |
|---------------|-----------|-------|
| Routine operations | Simple majority (5/9) | Monthly spending, minor policy |
| Major decisions | Supermajority (6/9) | Board chair election, policy changes, veto of platform decision |
| Emergency action | 2/3 majority (6/9) | Security breach, critical bug, financial irregularity |
| Removal of member | Supermajority (6/9) | Plus 30-day notice + hearing |
| Charter amendment | Unanimous (9/9) | Only if rare foundational change needed |

**Voting Process:**
1. Chair opens discussion
2. Members present positions (3 min per person max)
3. Chair restates issue for clarity
4. Secret ballot (ensures no coercion)
5. Secretary records tally + each member's position (but not their identity)
6. Result announced + documented

## 🛑 Board Veto & Override Powers

### **What the Board Can Veto**

The Cross-Platform Governance Board can block ANY decision by any single platform if it violates ecosystem-wide **Ethical Constitution**:

**Board can veto:**
- ✅ Pay-to-win mechanics added to Ascendant (violates cosmetic-only doctrine)
- ✅ Selling user data from 3mpwrApp (violates privacy-first principle)
- ✅ Foundation grant allocation that violates disability-led principle
- ✅ Major partnerships that conflict with mission
- ✅ Founder unilateral decision that affects all three (must involve board)

**Board cannot veto:**
- ❌ Day-to-day moderation decisions by User Council
- ❌ Feature prioritization chosen by platform (within ethical bounds)
- ❌ Community event planning by local admins
- ❌ User support responses

**Veto Process:**
1. Any board member notices potential violation
2. Files **Ethical Review Request** with documentation
3. Ethics Committee investigates (1 week)
4. Full board votes (6/9 supermajority to veto)
5. If vetoed: Platform must revise within 30 days
6. Founder has right to appeal to full board (must persuade 7/9 to overturn)

## 💰 Financial Authority & Reporting

### **Board's Financial Role**

1. **Quarterly Allocation Votes** (Finance Committee recommends, board approves 6/9)
   - S0vryn9 revenue split: Platform infrastructure → Foundation → Founder/staff
   - Example allocation:
     - 40% to 3mpwrApp infrastructure + moderation
     - 30% to Ascendant infrastructure + development
     - 20% to Mutual Aid Foundation
     - 10% to founder (or saved for emergency fund / hiring)

2. **All Spending Over $X Requires Board Approval**
   - Define threshold (suggestion: $5,000 USD / monthly)
   - Founder must present business case
   - Board votes (5/9 simple majority)
   - Exceptions: Salary payroll, critical infrastructure patches (emergency founders call founder only)

3. **Quarterly Public Transparency Report**
   - Board publishes anonymized financials quarterly
   - Breakdown: Revenue by platform, expenses, foundation allocations, runway estimate
   - Accessible to all community members
   - Non-sensitive version only (no founder salary details)

4. **Annual Audit** (Board-commissioned)
   - Hire external auditor or conduct internal deep review
   - Check: No financial misconduct, all records accurate, allocations as voted
   - Report to board + public summary

## 🚨 Emergency Authority

### **When Board Has Unilateral Power (No Time for 6/9 Vote)**

**Critical Infrastructure Issue:**
- Server down, data leak, security breach
- Action: Board chair + Backend Trustee respond immediately
- Report to full board within 24 hours
- Post-action review at next meeting

**Platform Crisis:**
- Widespread user harm, safety issue, major bug
- Action: Relevant platform User Council takes emergency action + board notified
- Board can override (6/9 vote) if response is disproportionate

## 📢 Public Communication & Transparency

### **What Gets Published (Quarterly)**

**Public Board Summary (Quarter End)**
- Major decisions made (what + why, anonymized)
- User feedback themes
- Strategic direction for next quarter
- Financials (anonymized, no sensitive data)
- Board members' names + roles (so community knows who represents them)

**What Stays Confidential**
- Personal data of any user
- Specific founder salary/compensation
- Confidential partnerships (until announced)
- Internal disputes (board disagreements kept private)
- Any emergency security response details (until resolved)

### **Community Townhalls**

**Quarterly (After board meetings)**
- Board chair presents quarterly summary
- Community members ask questions (live Q&A, moderated)
- Feedback collected for next quarter
- Recorded + transcript published

## 🔗 Relationship to User Councils

### **Hierarchy (Not Command Structure — Feedback Loop)**

```
Community Users
    ↓
Three User Councils
(3mpwrApp, Ascendant, Foundation)
    ↓
Cross-Platform Governance Board
(9 members: 3 per platform)
    ↓
Founder (S0vryn9)
```

**Actually operates like this:**

- **User Councils** run daily operations + real-time moderation
- **Governance Board** meets quarterly to align strategy + allocate funds
- **Founder** manages S0vryn9 infrastructure + reports to board (no autonomous power)
- **Community** provides feedback to councils → councils escalate to board

**If User Council disagrees with board decision:**
- Escalate to board for review
- Board reconsiders (must explain why they're keeping decision)
- User Council can appeal to community (call special town hall)

## ⚖️ Amendment & Dissolution

### **Charter Amendment**

**When can charter change?**
- Every 3 years (or if major structural issue found)
- Requires: Unanimous vote (9/9 board members)
- 30-day public notice before vote
- Community can petition for amendment (requires 10% community signatures)

**What cannot change** (immutable principles):
- Disability-led governance (PWD 2x voting weight)
- Quarterly transparency reporting
- Veto power over ethical violations
- Founder quarterly reporting requirement

### **Board Dissolution** (Extreme Scenario)

**If board is deemed failed:**
- Requires: 8/9 vote (near-universal)
- Triggers: Emergency User Council election within 14 days
- Founder temporarily manages until new governance formed

## ✅ Success Metrics

The board is functioning well if:
- ✅ Quarterly meetings happen on schedule (100% attendance or excused)
- ✅ Financial reports are transparent and timely (every quarter)
- ✅ User surveys show trust in leadership (>70% confidence)
- ✅ No major ethical violations go unaddressed
- ✅ Platform retention + growth metrics improve or stabilize
- ✅ Community submits feedback (low engagement = disengagement risk)
