## 🌟 The Viral Feature Stack

### 1. Daily Constellation Challenge ⭐ **PRIMARY VIRAL DRIVER**

**The Concept:**
- Every player worldwide gets THE SAME procedurally-generated ritual each day
- Share spoiler-free emoji/sigil grids (Wordle-style)
- Global leaderboard celebrating creativity, not just speed
- Creates daily community touchpoint

**Why It Goes Viral:**
- ✅ Social proof (everyone's doing it)
- ✅ FOMO (limited-time challenge)
- ✅ Non-spoiler sharing (safe to post)
- ✅ Comparison without toxicity (multiple success metrics)
- ✅ Daily habit formation

### 2. Ritual Replay Sharing 📹 **SECONDARY VIRAL DRIVER**

**The Concept:**
- Auto-generate 6-second gorgeous animations of ritual completions
- Stunning particle effects, sigil transformations, glowing trails
- One-tap share to TikTok, Instagram Reels, Twitter
- Each ritual type produces unique visual

**Why It Goes Viral:**
- ✅ No effort required (auto-generated)
- ✅ Beautiful aesthetics (Instagram-worthy)
- ✅ Shows gameplay without spoiling puzzles
- ✅ Platform-native format (vertical 9:16 video)
- ✅ Branded watermark (free advertising)

---

### 3. Personal Sigil Creation 🎨 **IDENTITY HOOK**

**The Concept:**
- Combine collected sigils to create YOUR unique magical signature
- Procedurally generated based on playstyle and choices
- Leave sigil traces in realms for other players to discover
- Visual representation of your journey

**Why It Goes Viral:**
- ✅ Personal expression (players love customization)
- ✅ Permanence (legacy in game world)
- ✅ Screenshot-worthy (show off your creation)
- ✅ Identity formation ("this is MY sigil")

**Why It Goes Viral:**
- ✅ FOMO ("I got the rare thing!")
- ✅ Bragging rights
- ✅ Stunning visuals
- ✅ Surprise factor (no one expects it)

- Provide unique ritual combinations for exclusive content
- Encourage Daily Challenge participation

**2. Reddit Presence**
- r/AndroidGaming, r/iOSGaming, r/IndieGaming
- Post about accessibility-first design
- Share dev journey (transparent, authentic)
- Respond to community feedback

**3. TikTok Strategy**
- Create official account
- Post ritual replay highlights
