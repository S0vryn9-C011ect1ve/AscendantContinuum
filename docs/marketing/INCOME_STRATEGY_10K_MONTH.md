## 🚨 Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Consulting Clients Dry Up
**Mitigation:**
- Build email list of past clients (monthly newsletter with tips)
- Create productized service (fixed scope, fixed price) to reduce sales friction
- Maintain Upwork/Fiverr presence even when busy

### Risk 2: Game Doesn't Monetize
**Mitigation:**
- Don't rely on game revenue alone (diversify into consulting + content)
- Test willingness to pay with 50 beta testers before scaling marketing
- Pivot to B2B licensing if consumer monetization fails

### Risk 3: Burnout from Multiple Income Streams
**Mitigation:**
- Automate everything possible (AI tools, Zapier, scripts)
- Hire part-time VA ($500/month) once hitting $3K/month revenue
- Focus on 2-3 streams max at any given time (don't spread too thin)

### Risk 4: Time Sinks (Distractions)
**Mitigation:**
- Track time rigorously (Toggl or Clockify)
- Kill any activity that doesn't directly generate revenue or traction
- Say no to unpaid podcast interviews, coffee chats, "pick your brain" requests

- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** Track growth week-over-week

- **Hours Per Dollar:** How many hours to earn $1? (Target: < 2 hours)

- **AI Time Savings:** How many hours does AI save you per week? (Target: 10+)
