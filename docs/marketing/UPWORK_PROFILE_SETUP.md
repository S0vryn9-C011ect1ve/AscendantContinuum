## 💡 Pro Tips

### Proposal Best Practices
1. **Respond fast:** Within 2 hours of job posting = 3x higher chance
2. **Ask a question:** Shows you read their requirements
3. **Show proof:** Link to your GitHub, live demo, or screenshots
4. **Price clearly:** Hourly estimate OR fixed price (not both)
5. **Be specific:** "I'll deliver X, Y, Z in N days"

### Red Flags to Avoid
❌ Jobs asking for free work ("Send me a sample first")  
❌ Jobs with zero budget ("We'll pay after launch")  
❌ Jobs requiring NDA before even talking  
❌ Jobs with vague requirements ("Make a game like Fortnite")  
❌ Jobs paying < $15/hour (not worth your time)

### Green Flags to Pursue
✅ Clear scope ("Integrate Firebase Auth into existing Unity project")  
✅ Reasonable budget ($500-$2,000 range)  
✅ Client has payment verified  
✅ Client has hired before (check history)  
✅ Job posted in last 24 hours
