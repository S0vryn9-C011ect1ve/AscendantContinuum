Getting Started

Your first session in The Ascendant Continuum

The Ascendant Continuum is a relaxing puzzle-adventure across five magical realms. Sessions are 1–5 minutes — designed to fit around fatigue, pain, and busy days. Here's how a first session flows.

Your First Session

  1. Arrive. You open in a calm hub. No timer, no pressure.
  2. Choose a realm. Each of the five realms has its own gentle puzzle logic and color story.
  3. Solve at your pace. Puzzles are about noticing, not speed. There is no fail state that punishes you.
  4. Collect sparks. Small completions leave sparks you can revisit later when energy is low.
  5. Optional community. When ready, leave a note on the Wish Wall or pass a Kindness Chain.

Accessibility From the Start

Open Settings in-game to turn on colorblind palettes, high-contrast, reduced-motion, or larger text. See the Accessibility page for the full list.
