Community Guidelines

The Ascendant Continuum — kindness is the game

The Ascendant Continuum is a gentle, wonder-first space. Our social features — the Wish Wall and the Kindness Chain — are designed for encouragement, not competition. These guidelines keep that promise.

Do

  • Share kind words, small wins, and encouragement on the Wish Wall.
  • Pass a Kindness Chain forward — lift someone else when you are lifted.
  • Respect differing abilities, energy levels, and pacing.
  • Use content warnings where a story touches on heavy topics.

Don't

  • No harassment, hate speech, or targeted negativity — anywhere.
  • No spam, scams, or self-promotion that drowns out others.
  • No sharing of another person's private information.
  • No bullying disguised as "feedback."

Moderation

Reports are reviewed by a small human team. Repeated or severe violations may result in removal from social features. We moderate for safety, not for silence — good-faith disagreement is welcome.
