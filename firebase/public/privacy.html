Privacy Policy

The Ascendant Continuum — Last updated May 4, 2026

1. Who We Are

The Ascendant Continuum (“we”, “our”, “the game”) is developed by AscendantContinuum (contact: ascendantcontinuum@gmail.com). This policy explains what data we handle and, more importantly, what we do not.

2. Our Privacy Commitment

We do not collect gameplay analytics, behavioural data, session data, crash telemetry, or any form of usage tracking. No Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, no advertising IDs, no third-party tracking pixels. Your gameplay is yours alone.

3. Data We Handle

  • Waitlist email address — Only if you voluntarily submit your email via the website sign-up form. Used solely to notify you when the game launches. Never sold or shared. Unsubscribe at any time by emailing us.
  • Local save data — Progress (sparks, sigils, achievements) is stored entirely on your device. We never read it.
  • Optional anonymous cloud save — If you choose to enable cloud save in Settings, your progress is stored in Firebase Firestore under a randomly generated anonymous ID. This ID cannot be linked to you as a person. You may delete it at any time.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Gameplay behaviour, realm visits, session duration, or any in-game actions
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or hardware fingerprints
  • Crash reports or performance telemetry
  • Location data of any kind
  • Financial or payment information (handled entirely by Apple App Store / Google Play)
  • Camera, microphone, or contacts
  • Any data from children under 13

5. AI-Powered Features (Realm Dwellers)

The game includes Realm Dwellers — characters whose dialogue evolves through an aggregated collective memory system. When cloud save is enabled, anonymised in-game interactions (e.g. wishes left on the Wish Wall, Kindness Chain completions) may contribute to the shared world state that shapes Realm Dweller responses. This process uses no personal data and cannot be attributed to any individual. You may opt out entirely by keeping cloud save disabled.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Firestore (Google) — Used only for waitlist email collection and optional anonymous cloud save. Firebase Analytics is explicitly disabled. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — Handle all payment processing. We receive no payment data. Google Privacy Policy  |  Apple Privacy Policy

7. GDPR & Your Rights (EU / UK Residents)

The legal basis for processing your waitlist email is your explicit consent (opt-in form submission). You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data at any time. To remove your email from our waitlist or delete your anonymous cloud save, contact us and we will action the request within 30 days.

8. COPPA (Children Under 13)

The game is rated 4+ / Everyone. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9. Data Retention

Waitlist emails are retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Anonymous cloud save data is retained until you delete the app or request deletion. We hold no other personal data.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at ascendantcontinuum@gmail.com.
