The Journey

What is the Continuum?

🌌

Celestial Realms

Explore five distinct realms — Emberforge, Verdant, Echo Fields, Dawn Citadel, and Lantern Ascension — each with its own living ecosystem and mysteries.

✨

Cosmic Identity

Your name is written in the stars. Build a unique cosmic identity that evolves through your play patterns, sigil choices, and alignment with lunar cycles.

🌿

Mindful Play

The game rewards you for taking breaks. A Digital Sunset system gently closes the app at dusk, encouraging you to witness the real sky.

🌙

Live Cosmic Events

Real-world lunar phases, meteor showers, and eclipses shift the game world. Step outside during a real shower for a Cosmic Meditation Bonus.

♿

Accessible by Design

Colorblind modes, reduced motion, dyslexia-friendly text, haptics control, and ADHD session reminders — built in from the start, not as an afterthought.

🤝

Cross-Player Wish Wall

Leave a wish for another traveller. Receive one from a stranger across the continuum. Connection without competition.
