AI & Disability Representation — What the Research Says

2026-09-12 · R&D Notes · S0vryn9 Research Watch
AI EthicsDisabilityAccessibility

Two recent arXiv papers inform Ascendant Continuum's design philosophy — a game built for the full disability community, where accessibility is the default, not an afterthought.

1. "Investigating the Representation of Disability Through the Lens of LLMs" (arXiv:2605.20191) — documents how large language models encode disability bias. Relevant to Ascendant's AI-assisted narrative and companion systems: we must train/tune for accurate, non-stereotypical representation.

2. "Disability, Job Satisfaction, and Workplace Accommodations" (arXiv:2602.10403 / 2602.20327) — quantifies accommodation request rates and approval gaps. Informs the evidence templates shared across the S0vryn9 ecosystem (3mpwrapp).
