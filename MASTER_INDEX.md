### 10 Things Never Done Before:

1. **Accessibility-Gated Secrets** - Different colorblind modes unlock DIFFERENT content
2. **Archaeological Player History** - Past rituals become discoverable fossils
3. **NPC Collective Memory** - NPCs evolve from ALL player conversations globally
4. **Long-Term Community Mysteries** - 6-12 month solve times (intentional)
5. **Cross-Player Puzzle Chains** - Async collaboration across 10-100 players
6. **Time Capsule System** - Messages discovered 30-365 days later

8. **Ethical Treasure Hunting** - Fully accessible with audio/visual/haptic cues
9. **Real-World Event Sync** - Solar eclipses trigger in-game mysteries
